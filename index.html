Three Pillars of Solar Purification

Reverse Osmosis

High-pressure membran separation for saline and ocean water.

  • Removes dissolved salts and heavy metals
  • Ideal for seawater and brackish groundwater
  • Driven by solar pump arrays

Ultra-filtration

0.02-micron barrier that filters turbidity with minimal energy.

  • Removes 99.9999% bacteria and prasites
  • Best for rivers , lakes , and catchment ponds
  • Ultra-low pressure direct DC drive

Solar Thermal Distillation

Vacuum evaporation powered by solar thermal heat exchangers

  • Yields pure distilled water with zero consumables
  • Proccesses hyper-saline feed and chemicals
  • Direct thermal capture via solar tubes

Frequently Asked Questions

How do systems operate during cloudy days and nights?

System run at high throughput during sumlight hours to fill elevated clean water storage tanks, allowing gravity distribution 24/7.

What maintenance is requried by operators?

Automated self-cleaning cycles handle daily needs, with only a monthly check of the pre-filter mesh.

Can Ultrafiltration turn seawater into drinking water?

No, Ultragiltration removes bacteria and sediment, while Reverse Osmosis or Thermal units are required for ocean salts.

How long do the solar panels and filters lasts

Solar panels last 25 years, while membranes typically last 3 to 8 years depending on usage.
